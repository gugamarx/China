Last updated: November 20, 2026

Simple guarantee: if you're not happy, we refund 100% within 14 calendar days of purchase. No reason needed.

1. All customers: 14-day refund

You can request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase, for a technical problem or simply because you're not satisfied.

2. EU / UK consumers

The game is personalized digital content generated and delivered right after payment. At checkout you request immediate delivery and acknowledge that, as a result, the statutory right of withdrawal may no longer apply once the game has been delivered. We still voluntarily offer the 14-day full refund described above.

3. Brazil

Brazilian customers also have the statutory 7-day right of withdrawal for online purchases (Consumer Protection Code, Art. 49), which our 14-day guarantee already covers.

4. How to request

Email gustavoomarques@gmail.com with the email address used for the purchase (and the order number, if you have it). We reply within 2 business days. If your purchase was processed by Paddle or Dodo Payments, you can also request the refund directly through them using the link in your receipt.

5. How you get your money back

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method.
  • Card refunds usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • For international purchases processed by our reseller (Merchant of Record), the refund is issued by the reseller.

6. What happens to the game

After a refund, the game link is deactivated and the photos are deleted from our servers.

7. Exceptions

We may decline refunds in cases of proven fraud or clear abuse (for example, repeated purchases and refunds of the same game). This never limits your statutory rights.
