Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

MyKidGame (including the NoelGame product) is an independent project operated by Gustavo de Oliveira Marques, a self-employed freelance software developer (individual, not incorporated) based in Goiânia - GO, Brazil. Contact and Data Protection Officer: gustavoomarques@gmail.com.

In short: we only ask for what the game needs to work. The free-trial photo never leaves your phone. Photos uploaded after purchase live on a private link and are automatically deleted after 60 days. We don't sell data, we don't show ads, and we never use photos to train artificial intelligence.

1. Data controller

The data controller is the MyKidGame operator described above, in Brazil. For any request about your data, email gustavoomarques@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days.

2. Who the service is for

Our games are purchased and set up only by parents or legal guardians aged 18 or over. Children only play: they don't create accounts, don't type in any data and can't chat with anyone in the game.

3. What we collect

3.1 Free trial (preview)

The photo used in the free trial is processed only in your device's memory, inside the browser. It is never uploaded to our servers and disappears when you close the page.

3.2 After purchase

  • From the parent: email address (to send the game link and receipt).
  • About the child: first name or nickname, sex (M/F, used only for grammatical agreement in the chosen language) and the photos you upload.
  • Payment: we never see or store card details. The payment processor only tells us the status, amount and a transaction ID.

3.3 Waitlist

Email, theme of interest, language, approximate country (derived from your IP; the IP itself is not stored) and the date and version of your consent.

3.4 Technical data

Our host (Cloudflare) logs technical connection data such as IP address and browser for security and abuse prevention. We only use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. We don't use Google Analytics, social media pixels or other third-party trackers.

5. Parental consent (children)

We always process children's data in their best interest. Before any photo is uploaded to our servers, the adult must: (1) declare they are the child's parent or legal guardian, or have their express permission; (2) tick the specific consent for the use of the photos; and (3) complete a payment. This combination is a method of verifiable parental consent recognized under COPPA (USA) and meets GDPR Art. 8, whose age threshold ranges from 13 to 16 depending on the country.

6. How long we keep it

  • Photos, child's name and sex: up to 60 days after purchase, then automatically deleted. We email you a notice beforehand. If you choose the optional paid renewal, the period is extended for the time purchased.
  • Immediate deletion: your confirmation email includes a link that deletes the game and all photos instantly.
  • Waitlist: until you unsubscribe.
  • Sales records (email, amount, date, payment ID, no photos): for the period required by tax law, usually 5 years.
  • Offline copy you download: stays only on your device, under your control.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Photos are never used to train artificial intelligence models, by us or by these providers, and are never used for advertising. We only disclose data to authorities when legally required.

8. International transfers

Some providers are located outside Brazil and the EU, mainly in the USA. Transfers follow GDPR Chapter V and LGPD Art. 33, using standard contractual clauses and the safeguards these providers offer.

9. Security

We use HTTPS across the site, long random tokens in game links that can't be guessed, game pages excluded from search engines, encryption at rest provided by our hosts, and restricted access to data. No system is 100% secure; if a significant incident occurs, we will notify you and the authorities within the legal deadlines.

10. Your rights

At any time and free of charge you can: confirm whether we process your data, access it, correct it, request portability, request erasure (right to be forgotten), object, withdraw consent and learn who we share it with (GDPR Arts. 15–21; LGPD Art. 18). Parents and guardians can also review and delete their child's data and refuse further use (COPPA). To exercise these rights, use the deletion link in your email or write to gustavoomarques@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, or with Brazil's ANPD.

11. Region-specific notes

  • EU and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): the rights above include erasure and objection.
  • USA (COPPA): we do not collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent, and we don't condition a child's play on collecting more data than necessary.
  • Japan (APPI): we use data only for the stated purpose, with the legal representative's consent, and do not provide it to third parties beyond those listed in section 7.

12. Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The site only stores your language choice in your browser (localStorage).

13. Changes

If we make an important change, we'll update the date at the top and email anyone with an active game.
